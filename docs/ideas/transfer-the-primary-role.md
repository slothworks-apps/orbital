# Hand the primary role to another member

Only the primary adds and removes devices
([[the-group-has-one-primary-device]]). When the person replaces that Mac,
or wants a server to manage the group, they now leave and found a new
group, pairing every device again.

A handover from the living primary would keep the group: the primary
signs a last version naming the new primary, and the new one signs every
version after it. Members take the change only from the old primary's
signature, so a stolen member cannot claim the role. The phone cannot
take the role.
