# The phone's short messages get their own one-place design

The Mac now shows every short message — replies to the user's actions and
notices about the app — in one queue between the panels
([[2026-10-09-one-place-for-messages-design]]). The phone was left out on
purpose: it says request failures on the composer's line, shows no `info`
replies at all, and pins its notices above the session list.

Worth designing on its own terms: whether the phone wants replies at all,
where they sit on a small screen, and how they meet the pinned notice.
