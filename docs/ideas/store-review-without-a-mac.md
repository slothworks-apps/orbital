# Store review of the phone app without a Mac

The phone app does nothing until it is paired with a Mac running Orbital,
through the relay. An App Store or Google Play reviewer has neither, and
could not set one up without a Claude subscription. Both stores ask for
the whole app to be reviewable (Apple's guideline 2.1, Google Play's "App
access").

## The idea

Do what companion apps for hardware and remote-access apps do: submit a
video and review notes.

- **The video** is recorded on a real phone paired with a Mac. It shows:
  pairing by scanning the QR code, the session list, a session that needs
  input and the answer to it, a harness gate approved, a new session
  started, and a push notification arriving while the app is closed.
- **The data in it is staged.** The Mac runs Orbital against a separate
  `ORBITAL_CLAUDE_DIR` and `ORBITAL_DATA_DIR` holding demo projects and
  sessions, so no real project, path or conversation appears on screen.
- **The review notes** say the app is a companion to the Orbital desktop
  app, why it cannot be paired during review (it needs a Mac running
  Orbital and a Claude subscription), and link the video. Apple takes them
  in App Store Connect's App Review Information, Google Play in App access.

If Apple still answers that it cannot review the app, the video and notes
go again through the Resolution Center. That usually costs one round, not
a different approach.

## Not now: a demo mode in the app

A "Try without a Mac" link on the pairing screen could open the app on
local fixture data with no network: a fake transport in `web/src/mobile`
in place of the relay client. Review does not need it. It would earn its
place as onboarding for people who install the phone app before the
desktop app, or for store screenshots, and it is a second path through the
app that every phone change has to keep working.

## Ruled out

- **A hosted demo Mac paired with the reviewer.** A reviewer could start
  Claude sessions on the owner's machine and subscription, and pairing only
  works by scanning the Mac's QR code (pasting a code is dev-build only).

## Open questions

- Whether the staged demo data is written by hand or generated by a
  script that can be rerun before each submission whose screens changed.
