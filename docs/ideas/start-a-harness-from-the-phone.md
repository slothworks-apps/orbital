# Start a harness, go back and remove from the phone

[[2026-10-06-harness-in-the-timeline-design]] lets the phone follow a
harness, answer its gates and pause or resume it. Starting one, going
back to a step and removing it stay on the Mac: starting needs the
template list, scopes and inputs; go back and remove are rare and
destructive.

Worth doing once harnesses are started away from the desk often enough
to miss it: a short template picker (this project's templates first),
inputs as plain fields, and go back / remove behind a confirm sheet.
Needs the start, go-back and delete routes on the allowlist.
