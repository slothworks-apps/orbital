# Code of conduct

Orbital follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else you
speak for or about the project.

In short: be considerate, assume good intent, criticise the work and not the
person, and accept a maintainer's decision on what belongs in the project.
Harassment, personal attacks and publishing someone's private information
are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to support@slothworks.io. Reports are read by the
maintainer only and kept confidential. The maintainer decides on the
response, from a warning to a permanent ban, following the Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.
